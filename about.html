The Human Behind the Ecosystems.

I am Anthony Nduka (Annaelechukwu)—a strategist, developer, and community architect dedicated to driving Web3 adoption and empowering the next generation of builders across Africa.

I bridge the gap between complex tech and the people who use it.

"Community is not just a Discord server. It is a highly strategic growth engine."

My journey started at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), where I graduated with a BSc in Computer Science. I quickly realized that building great technology wasn't enough—someone had to translate that technology into real-world utility and adoption.

That realization led me to co-found BlockchainUNN, which grew into a powerhouse campus Web3 developer community. From there, I took on roles that required massive scale: training over 5,000 members for FTX Africa[cite: 5], serving as a regional Chapter Lead for CMX, and driving the strategy that onboarded 2M+ users to Celo's Minipay[cite: 4].

Today, I serve as the Head of Marketing and Growth at cKash, a self-custodial digital payments wallet operating on the Celo blockchain. Under my leadership, we successfully secured the Prezenti Almond Grant to aggressively expand stablecoin utility across Africa.

While growth marketing is my day-to-day, my roots remain technical. I still actively build—whether that's developing full-stack web applications, architecting off-grid IoT smart agricultural systems (like AVATAR), or creating Web3 audio-visual digital art experiences.
